Privacy Policy

Your trust matters. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how we keep it safe. Last updated: June 2026.

Note for the store owner: replace the bracketed placeholders below with your legal business name, address, and country, and have it reviewed for your jurisdiction before launch.

Who we are

Pawnest ("we", "us") operates this store. For privacy questions, contact hello@pawnest.shop [your registered business name & address].

What we collect

  • Order details — your name, email, shipping address, and phone number, so we can fulfill and deliver your order.
  • Payment data — handled entirely by our payment processor (e.g. Stripe or PayPal). We never see or store your full card number.
  • Usage data — basic analytics (pages viewed, device type) to improve the store, if analytics are enabled.

How we use it

  • To process, fulfill, and deliver your orders and provide support.
  • To send order and shipping updates (and, only with your consent, occasional offers).
  • To prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

Only the partners needed to run the store: our payment processor, and the suppliers/carriers who ship your order. We never sell your personal data.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing hello@pawnest.shop. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA.

Cookies

We use essential cookies/local storage to remember your cart. Any analytics or marketing cookies are only used with your consent.

Data retention & security

We keep order data only as long as needed for the order and legal requirements, and we use reputable, encrypted services to protect it.
