# 🎬 Pawnest — Faceless TikTok Script Pack

Ready-to-shoot scripts for a **faceless** pet store account. No camera, no face — just
licensed B-roll, on-screen text, captions, and trending audio. Built around your real
catalog so you can post on day one.

---

## ⚖️ First: where to get footage you're ALLOWED to use

Do **not** download other creators' TikToks/YouTube clips and re-post them — that's
copyright infringement and gets accounts banned. Use these instead (all free, all
commercially licensed):

| Source | License | Best for |
|--------|---------|----------|
| **Your supplier's media** | Most AliExpress/CJ/Zendrop suppliers provide product photos & videos for dropshippers — confirm it's permitted on the listing | The actual product in use |
| **Pexels Videos** (pexels.com/videos) | Free, no attribution, commercial OK | Dogs, cats, homes, lifestyle B-roll |
| **Pixabay** (pixabay.com/videos) | Free, no attribution, commercial OK | Pet & lifestyle clips |
| **Mixkit** (mixkit.co) | Free Mixkit License, commercial OK | Polished pet/lifestyle shots |
| **Coverr** (coverr.co) | Free, commercial OK | Cozy home/ambient backgrounds |
| **Your own phone** | You own it | Best-performing — film the product once samples arrive |

> Rule of thumb: if you didn't film it, get it from a royalty-free library above or
> from your supplier with permission. When using Creative Commons clips that *require*
> attribution, credit the creator in your caption.

**Free editing + voice tools:** CapCut (free; text, captions, auto-captions, AI
text-to-speech voices), TikTok's native editor, ElevenLabs (free tier) for voiceover.
For trending audio, always pick a **sound trending in TikTok's library that day** (tap
the audio → "Add to Favorites" while it's rising) — I note the *style* of audio below.

## 🧩 Faceless formats that work for pet products
1. **Problem → Product → Relief** — show the pain (fur everywhere / dog pulling), then the fix.
2. **Satisfying B-roll + text** — calm aesthetic clips + a list of benefits, ASMR-style audio.
3. **"Things my dog/cat needed" listicle** — 3–5 products, fast cuts.
4. **POV / relatable text hook** — text on a cozy clip, no product until the payoff.
5. **Before/After** — messy couch → clean couch; anxious pet → sleeping pet.

Each script below = **Hook (0–3s) → Body shots → Payoff → CTA**. Keep total **8–18s**.
Put 3–6 word text on screen, never cover the bottom 1/4 (UI lives there).

## 📜 The scripts

### 1. Calming Donut Bed — "anxious dog finally sleeps"
- **Format:** Problem → Relief · **Length:** ~12s
- **Hook text (0–3s):** "POV: your rescue can't settle at night 😢"
- **Shots:**
  - 0–3s: B-roll of a restless dog pacing / lying awake (Pexels: "anxious dog")
  - 3–7s: product clip — dog stepping into the donut bed, circling, curling up
  - 7–12s: close-up of dog fully asleep, slow zoom
- **On-screen text beats:** "Then we tried the calming bed" → "The raised rim = security" → "Out cold in 5 minutes 😴"
- **Voiceover (optional):** "If your dog struggles to settle, the raised rim mimics the feeling of being held — and it knocks them right out."
- **Audio style:** soft, cozy, slowed-down trending sound
- **CTA:** "🐾 Calming bed → link in bio"
- **Caption:** "Anyone else's rescue have the zoomies at midnight? 😅 #dogtok #rescuedog #puppytok #dogsoftiktok #petproducts"

## 📈 Free growth playbook
- **Post 1–3x/day.** Volume beats perfection early — most videos flop, a few pop.
- **First 3 seconds decide everything.** Hook hard, show motion immediately.
- **Hook the comments:** ask a question ("Cat or dog person?") to drive engagement.
- **Reply to every comment with a video** ("answering @user") — free reach.
- **Trend-jack:** use sounds that are *rising* (under ~10k videos), not peaked.
- **Funnel:** bio link → your store; pin your best-performing video.
- **Repurpose** the exact same clips to Instagram Reels & YouTube Shorts.
- **Whitelist disclosure:** if a post is an ad/paid partnership, mark it as such per TikTok rules.

> Keep a simple content calendar: 8 scripts × 3 hook variations × 3 platforms = 72 posts
> before you ever repeat yourself.
