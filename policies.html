Shipping & Returns

Everything you need to know about getting your order — and what happens if it's not perfect.

Shipping

  • Processing: orders are processed within 1–2 business days.
  • Delivery: most orders arrive in 7–15 business days depending on your location.
  • Free shipping on orders over $40. Otherwise a flat $4.99.
  • Tracking: you'll receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.
  • We ship worldwide. Customs or import fees, where they apply, are the buyer's responsibility.

Returns & refunds

  • 30-day returns. Not in love with it? Contact us within 30 days of delivery.
  • Items should be unused and in original packaging where possible.
  • Damaged or wrong item? Send us a photo and we'll replace it or refund you — no need to ship it back.
  • Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days of approval.

FAQ

Why does shipping take longer than Amazon?

We work directly with specialist suppliers to keep prices fair and our range wide, so items ship from their warehouses. We'd rather be upfront about timing than overpromise.

Where is my order?

Check the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email. Still unsure? Email us your order name and we'll chase it down.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Reach out within 12 hours of ordering and we'll do our best to update or cancel it before it ships.

How do I get help?

Email hello@pawnest.shop — a real person replies, usually within one business day.
