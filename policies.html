How we pick

No guesswork, no pay-to-play. Here's exactly how a product earns a spot on Pickwise — and how we make money doing it.

Our method

  • Ratings floor. We start with products rated 4.5★ or higher with a large, credible volume of reviews.
  • Review quality. We read the critical reviews, not just the glowing ones, and watch for fake-review patterns.
  • Value & durability. We weigh price against what you actually get, and favor products with a track record of holding up.
  • Real use. We prioritize items with broad, repeat-buyer demand over one-week viral fads.
  • We refresh. Picks are reviewed regularly; if a product's quality or rating slips, it comes off the list.
