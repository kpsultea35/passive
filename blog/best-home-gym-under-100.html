How to build from here

  • Start with the dumbbell + bands. Together they cover ~90% of a beginner-to-intermediate full-body routine.
  • Add a mat and a bench later. Those are the natural next upgrades once you're consistent.
  • Progressive overload is the point. The adjustable dumbbell exists so you can keep adding weight as you get stronger — use it.
  • Check weight range, not just price. Make sure the top end of the dumbbell leaves you room to grow.
