What to look for in a kitchen gadget

  • Capacity matches your household. A 6-quart air fryer suits a family; singles can size down and save.
  • Dishwasher-safe parts. If it's a pain to clean, you'll stop using it. Easy cleanup is the #1 predictor of a gadget you keep.
  • Read the critical reviews. Check the 1–3 star reviews for recurring complaints (smell, durability, noise) before you buy.
  • Don't overpay for "smart." App control is rarely worth a big premium on a kitchen device.
